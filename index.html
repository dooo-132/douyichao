About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Mathematics at Tsinghua University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, representation learning, and recommender systems, with a particular focus on generative recommendation and discrete semantic representations.

I am interested in exploring how mathematical structures and learning algorithms can contribute to effective and scalable intelligent systems.

Research Experience

Semantic ID Construction for Generative Recommendation

My research explores semantic identification methods for generative recommendation systems, including discrete representation learning, vector quantization, and clustering-based approaches.

I have investigated different Semantic ID construction strategies, including residual quantization, RQ-VAE, and spectral clustering-based methods.

I am also interested in multi-granularity semantic representations and their integration into sequential generative recommendation frameworks.
